From the refinery:

From the side country to the backcountry, a tour that group of youngster share with the new mountain range. Foremost the region is hard to reach for and our guide is as awesome as man who have dominated this sport for decades. The kindness, caring and strong she is, as well as natural beauty with the perfect movement and standing from great command on her boarding.

The way as a tall kind of girl, slim but strong…, appeal more to these girls that define the best out of anyone, and that is a pro, with creative, energetic and most of all, cool. Soon the status if they know they have all earn their turns and make the most of each run, awesome run and unbelievable geometrics, biometric higher than most peaks they run on.., turning in high speed pulling out the turns, and spins, each with form, is unbelievable.

The legacy of those lines, the mountain did no justice, does no harm's way…. As the way the acknowledgement of a society, and the common goal to contour the range as freestyle and ride solitary..with you or another friend, your soul mate, the one who saves you, maybe.

The intended character should be a young woman who need to come to term with herself with her wish to start a family, and with the struggle between her life in the mountains and the county she grown up in Japan, a distance that she wants from knowing she rather travel the world then seeking love in her life.

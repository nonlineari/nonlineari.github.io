Book of Formations

Artist
JS-Horseman
Released
25 Jul 2019
Format
Digital Album · Streaming + Download

Experimental composer, sound artist, visual artist, J S-Horseman (Jeremy Santiago-Horseman) works with microphone recording, modular synthesis, noise, acoustic instrumental and vocal composition, with emphasis on the recording formats of tape concrete and experimental vinyl recording.

The ambient experimental album by JS-Horseman was produced in 2017. JS use tapes manipulation and recording device in analogue machines to produce a sound that airy echo through the thin atmosphere to create an ambient sound of strange structural landscapes composition of earthly tones.

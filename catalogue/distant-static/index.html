Distant Static

Artist
ITEM
Released
29 Apr 2021
Format
Digital Album · Streaming + Download

Distant static is a collection of tracks created over a period of six months. It documents a time of freeform experimentation creating patches on digital and analog synthesizers, sometimes mixed with field recordings. The results are some moody ambient and drone pieces or more rythmic loop based compositions.

ITEM is a French producer and sound designer. He lives in Copenhagen.

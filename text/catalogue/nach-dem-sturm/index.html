Nach Dem Sturm

AMBIENT · 2020 · ambient, ambient beats, ambient experimental, downtempo, experimental ambient

Artist: Naked Nuras Loves Rave

Released: 16 May 2020

Format: Digital Track · Streaming + Download

Nach Dem Sturm - translate as After the Storm. The feeling of solitude, evoking a time while making music during a huge typhoon thunderstorm, and when the recording is done while the storm lasted, sunrises and calmness' arrival.

Master Engineer - Matt Colton @ Metropolis London, U.K.
